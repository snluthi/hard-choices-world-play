How it works
15 years in Aurel

  1. Four times a year, the council meets.

    Residents ask for things. So do the farmers, the national government and Kestra, the town downstream.

  2. You can act on one of them.

    You have a couple of work crews and a tight budget. Anything you leave on the table carries on, and people remember it.

  3. Each year ends with a look ahead.

    You see what changed, what arrives before the next election, and what the town looks like 15 years ahead if you change nothing.

For classrooms

Something worth arguing about in class

Aurel is for students from 16, early-career graduates and anyone who wants to see how a decision made today plays out over fifteen years.

  • Trace a problem to its causeA fountain that runs dry today goes back to how much the farms pumped five years earlier.
  • Compare this year with 15 years aheadEach option shows what happens this year, before the next election and after it.
  • Decide with incomplete informationForecasts give you odds. A heatwave can still arrive in a year you didn't plan for.
  • Count the political costSome decisions that help the town in 15 years lose you votes at the next election.
