One year in Aurel

  1. The council meets four times a year.

    Spring, summer, autumn, winter. Residents, farmers, the nation and the wider world all want something from you.

  2. You can act on one thing.

    Crews and money run short. Whatever stays on the table keeps going, and people notice.

  3. Then the year closes.

    See what it brought, what lands before the vote, and where it leads long after you have gone.

For classrooms

Made for real conversations about trade-offs.

For students from 16, early-career graduates and anyone who wants to feel why good decisions are hard to make, and harder to explain.

  • See systems, not symptomsA dry fountain is really about the ground water under the farms.
  • Weigh now against laterEvery option shows what lands now, before the vote, and after.
  • Decide without the full pictureForecasts give odds, not promises. The weather doesn't read them.
  • Feel the politicsThe right call can cost you the vote. The popular one can cost the town.
